# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of apartments before renovation in Warsaw and online. Additional existing segments: small apartments and rental apartments. Polish, Ukrainian and Russian are equal supported languages.

## Product Purpose

Turn an apartment plan and the residents' needs into a functional layout, visual direction and a plan of further decisions. The website should generate qualified enquiries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Astro with Cloudflare Workers, D1, private R2 uploads and Turnstile. Existing routes, form payload, consent and private file handling must continue to work. Current approved price starts at 1490 zł, delivery takes 3–5 business days, one round of minor revisions is included. A concept is not construction documentation or supervision. Base scope and start-of-delivery conditions remain open product decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Need for Design. The user selected a calm architectural studio direction and explicitly approved a clearly labelled demonstration instead of a real client case. Practical, attentive, contemporary communication.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing SVG plans are illustrative demonstrations. No verified designer biography, portrait, testimonials or real final PDF were supplied. Do not fabricate those assets or treat demo dimensions as client facts.

## Product Principles

- Show the deliverable and explain decisions.
- State price and scope honestly.
- Keep the enquiry possible without a plan attachment.
- Preserve language equivalence and privacy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, mobile-first layouts, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion and localised form feedback.
